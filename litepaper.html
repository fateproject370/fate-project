1. Executive Summary

FATE Project is a founder-led digital project developing practical software tools and digital legacy concepts. FATE is the project’s fixed-supply BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain. Its first public token-related function is a read-only holder verification beta. No presale or project fundraising round has been conducted. A FATE/WBNB pool exists on PancakeSwap V3. Its existence does not establish independent demand.

3. Project Purpose

FATE Project develops practical digital tools while documenting its FATE token and project status publicly. Current public products include three Telegram tools plus the published Cost & Profit and Genesis Business Android applications. RUDAR remains in active development; digital legacy concepts remain planned research directions.

The project separates released tools, development work and longer-term concepts so that availability and token integration are not overstated.

5. Contract Capabilities

Supply

  • The complete supply was minted once during deployment.
  • No public or external mint function exists.
  • No additional supply can be minted through the deployed contract.
  • No public burn function exists.

Control and ERC-20 functions

  • The contract is not Ownable.
  • No owner or administrator role exists.
  • The ABI contains standard ERC-20 transfer and allowance functionality.

6. Current Supply and Distribution

The 30 July 2026 disclosure recorded the deployer holding 1,000,000 FATE (100% of supply). That is a historical snapshot, not a current balance. On 31 August 2026, GeckoTerminal displayed 8 holder addresses. Address count does not establish the number of independent people or wallet ownership. See the current holder balances on BscScan.

7. Current Token Function

Public beta

Read-only holder verification

The holder verification beta reads the connected wallet’s FATE balance through a read-only blockchain call in the user’s browser.

It does not transfer tokens, request token approval, request a message signature, access private keys, store wallet addresses or offer a reward.

13. Risks and Limitations

  • FATE Project is an early-stage, founder-led project.
  • Holder addresses may be related; their count does not establish independent ownership or demand. Review the holder list for current balances.
  • Reported liquidity is very low. Trades may have substantial price impact, and the displayed price is not a guaranteed execution or exit price.
  • There is no guarantee of future product or token integration.
  • There is no guarantee that FATE will have value.
  • No financial return, yield, price increase or profit is promised.
