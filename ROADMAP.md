# FATE Project Roadmap

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This roadmap separates current work from future plans. It does not state guaranteed delivery dates or outcomes.

## Current

- Verified FATE token contract.
- Public project and token documentation.
- Official website, Telegram channel and X account.
- Public ecosystem tools: Coin of Fate, World Capitals and Margin Calculator.
- Published Android products: Cost & Profit and Genesis Business.
- Read-only FATE holder verification beta.
- Active FATE/WBNB PancakeSwap V3 liquidity.
- RUDAR active development.

## Next

- Keep public documentation aligned with live product and liquidity status.
- Improve distribution and discoverability for published products.
- Continue standard/non-priority listing review with major crypto data platforms.
- Evaluate practical FATE token integrations without overstating unreleased functionality.
- Continue RUDAR development and testing.

## Long-term

- Digital legacy research.
- Possible NFC/NFT implementation.
- Possible community participation mechanisms.

Long-term items are research and planning directions. They are not released products, current token functions or assured commitments.
