# FATE Project Products

FATE Project currently focuses on practical software products, web tools and Telegram bots.

## Android applications

- **Cost & Profit** — cost, profit, margin and markup calculator for entrepreneurs and small businesses.
  - RuStore: https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.fateproject.costandprofit
- **Genesis Business** — structured support for complex business decisions, scenarios and risks.
  - RuStore: https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.fateproject.genesis
- **Design psychology project** — currently in development and preparation for a public version.

## Telegram bots

- **Монетка судьбы / Fate Coin** — random choice between two options.
  - https://t.me/monetka_sudby_bot
- **Столицы мира / World Capitals** — learning tool for world capitals.
  - https://t.me/capitals_world_game_bot
- **Калькулятор маржинальности / Margin Bot** — quick margin, markup and profit calculations.
  - https://t.me/margin_business_bot
