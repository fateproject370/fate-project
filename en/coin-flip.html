Frequently asked questions

Is this coin fair?

Yes. The result comes from your browser's random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues): heads and tails have exactly equal chances, and previous flips don't affect the next one.

How do I use a coin flip to make a decision?

Phrase your question so the answer is yes or no, decide in advance what heads means, and flip. If the result disappoints you, that is a hint too — about what you actually want.

Can I flip a coin in Telegram?

Yes. The @monetka_sudby_bot bot answers yes or no, can flip a coin for a friend via a link, and works right in any chat: type @monetka_sudby_bot followed by a space.
