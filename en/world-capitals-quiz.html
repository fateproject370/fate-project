Frequently asked questions

How many countries are in the quiz?

191 countries from every continent. Each round has 10 random questions, and the wrong options are capitals from the same continent, so it isn't too easy.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. In the Telegram bot @capitals_world_game_bot you can challenge a friend: they play the same 10 questions and you see who scored more. You can also send a question to any chat: type @capitals_world_game_bot followed by a space.

What is the fastest way to learn the capitals?

Learn by continent: start with Europe and the larger countries of Asia, then Africa and Oceania. Play a round every day — the bot tracks your daily streak and best score.

Is it free?

Yes. The quiz on this page and the main capitals game in the bot are free and unlimited.
